The clean control is independently initialized, while the corrected store is reconstructed from its real event journal.

Generated English and Turkish documents also pass through the actual proposal, source-revision check, create-only file publication, process crash, durable receipt recovery and a newly loaded model process. Runtime checks and human assessment of the saved prose remain separate.

These explicit quantity checks do not establish unrestricted semantic correctness.

## Learning and admission

Audit available real episodes before training: complete prompt/context, source/model identities, unique examples, family-level splits and independent verdicts. Teacher completion and host execution are distinct from correctness. Preserve rejected proposals and corrected replacements. Freeze the original BF16 instrument and candidate version, training data, trainable modules, precision, seeds, budgets and generation controls before each experiment. Training does not automatically activate a candidate.

A local adaptation tests whether this corpus and training path produce useful held-out improvement. It is a signal/feasibility experiment. Joint byte patching, recurrent state and nested capacity require their own matched ablations and general-language/creation replay. Measure generated complete tasks, retention, conduct, latency tails, RSS, physical footprint, swap and full preparation/output cost. Likelihood or agreement alone cannot qualify a continuing general agent. A negative candidate result leaves the broader retained architecture requirement open.
