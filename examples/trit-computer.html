Trit Computer

Three values, yes, no and maybe, carried by two binary bits. The page shows what the three values can do in a 3×3 junction, how they are stored and guarded, and how the third value computes π with exact bounds: the maybe cells are the unknown, carried as a value, and refinement shrinks them.

A trit holds log₂3 = 1.585 bits of information. Two bits per trit use 79.2% of their capacity and leave one code free as the guard. Packing five trits per byte (3⁵ = 243 of 256 codes) uses 99.1%, which is Codex's judgment codec: digits little-endian, unused high digits zero; its 13 spare codes are its guard, and they too miss every change from one valid code to another.

2. The 3×3 junction

Two judgments meet and combine; the result is again a trit, so junctions compose. Choose the operation the node names.

Unary NOT swaps yes and no and leaves maybe. Kleene's strong three-valued logic is the one where maybe means unknown; Łukasiewicz differs only at maybe→maybe, which it calls yes. The two arithmetic operations use a different typed alphabet: there all three values are known numbers, so −1 is a quantity and never an unknown, and a sum needs a carry trit because one trit cannot hold −2 or +2. A judgment alphabet and an arithmetic alphabet may share the same two-bit codes; they must never share a node.

Supersession, not mutation

A judgment node is immutable. Moving maybe to yes or no creates a new version with lineage; the old one stays as evidence.

3. π by three-valued judgment

Cut the unit quarter disk into n×n cells. Judge each cell: yes when its far corner is inside the circle, no when its near corner is outside, maybe otherwise. The yes cells are inside for certain, the maybe cells are the boundary band. Then

4·yes/n² ≤ π ≤ 4·(yes + maybe)/n²

Every count is an integer and every comparison is exact, so the bounds are true, not approximate. Refining n shrinks the maybe band, and the band's width bounds the error: 4·maybe/n² ≈ 8/n. π is bracketed ever more tightly and never reached, which is what the open value means.

Why the corner test is exact: the disk is convex, so a cell whose far corner is inside is wholly inside; and in the first quadrant the near corner is the cell's closest point to the origin, so a cell whose near corner is outside has no interior point of the disk.
